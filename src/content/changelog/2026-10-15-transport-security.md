Three recommended requirements cover the receiving side of authentication: [MTA-STS](/spec/authentication/mta-sts/) and [DANE for SMTP](/spec/authentication/dane-for-smtp/) so senders insist on valid TLS to your mail servers, and [TLS reporting](/spec/authentication/tls-reporting/) so you hear when that fails.
